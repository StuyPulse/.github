StuyPulse is a student-led robotics team competing in the First Robotics Competition (FRC). Founded in 2000, we bring together students from diverse backgrounds to design, build, and program competitive robots over a six-week long build season.

Our mission goes far beyond competition, fostering a welcoming, inclusive community where students can explore STEM, develop technical and leadership skills, and discover a passion for lifelong learning. Beyond our own team, we extend this mission by mentoring younger robotics teams, providing technical assistance to fellow FRC teams, and sharing our knowledge to help everyone grow and succeed.
